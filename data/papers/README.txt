PUT YOUR REAL RESEARCH PAPERS (PDF) IN THIS FOLDER
===================================================

1. Copy your PDFs here. Name them so the file name starts with a paper id:
       P1_Author_Title.pdf
       P2_Author_Title.pdf
       ...
   PaperPilot uses the part before the first underscore (P1, P2 ...) as the paper id.
   Stable ids keep your ground truth and your review decisions matched to the right paper.

2. (Optional but recommended) Write your answer key in this folder as ground_truth.json.
   Copy data/ground_truth_template.json and fill it in by reading the papers yourself.
   The Quality Report tab then measures claim coverage, contradiction precision and gap recall.

3. Start the app, then click "Load my papers (data/papers/)" in the sidebar.

Your review papers are never modified or deleted by PaperPilot. It only reads them.
